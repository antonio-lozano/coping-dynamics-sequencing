Fig 3B-H and Supp Fig 1 — Mixed LMM timecourse
  Python src/statistics.py :: fit_mixed_models(df, response_col)
    Per-experiment:
      smf.mixedlm("pct ~ condition * time_bin_numeric",
                  sub, groups=sub["animal_id"],
                  re_formula="1").fit(reml=False)
    Combined (both experiments):
      same + vc_formula={"experiment": "0 + C(experiment)"}
    condition reference = "Control"; time_bin_numeric in SECONDS (30...450)
    Returns: analysis, parameter, coef, std_err, z, p_value, ci_low, ci_high,
             n_observations, n_animals

  R equivalent (used in build_fig3_report.R for Excel output):
    Per-exp:  lmer(pct ~ group * time_s + (1|animal_id), REML=FALSE)
    Combined: lmer(pct ~ group * time_s + (1|animal_id) + (1|experiment), REML=FALSE)
    Key label: "groupELS:time_s" = "Stress x time" -- this is what's reported in
               Fig 3B-H manuscript (not the stress main effect)

  For Supp Fig 1 (Inaccurate tracking / Mix behaviors):
    Same pipeline BUT time is CENTERED at mean (240s) so that the stress main
    effect (groupELS) = ELS-Control difference at the MEAN timepoint:
      lmer(pct ~ group * time_c + (1|animal_id) + (0+1|experiment), REML=FALSE)
      where time_c = time_bin - 240
    Confirmed: groupELS beta=-2.341 (exact match to manuscript).
    SE=0.942 NOT REPRODUCED by any model -- our best gives SE=1.183.
